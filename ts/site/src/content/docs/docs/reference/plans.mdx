## Add-ons

{tiers.team.name} and {tiers.business.name} can add, each month:

- {formatCount(addOnPrices.startsBlock)} more starts for
  {formatMoney(addOnPrices.startsPrice)}.
- One more machine for {formatMoney(addOnPrices.machinePrice)}.

Set the add-ons in the checkout, or on **Plan and usage**. A change takes
effect at once, and Stripe prorates it for the rest of the period.

## What counts as a start

A start is one job that a ciproxy runner takes, on your machines or on
Boat, whatever its result.

- A job counts at the moment that a runner takes it.
- A job that runs again is a new start.
- A warm runner that takes no job is not a start.
- A Boat runner that takes no job is not a start.
- A job that no runner takes is not a start, for example a job that someone
  cancels while it waits.
- The starts of a workspace count the jobs of all its organizations.

The page of each job says "Counts as 1 start".

## Periods

- A paid plan counts the starts over its Stripe billing period, for example
  4 November to 3 December.
- {tiers.free.name} counts the starts over periods of one month from the day
  that the workspace starts on {tiers.free.name}. In a month without that
  day, the period starts on the last day of the month.
- The days and the dates of every period are in UTC.
- A move from {tiers.free.name} to a paid plan starts a new period when
  Stripe confirms the payment. A move between paid plans keeps the period.

## Soft limits

The starts and the machines of your plan, with its add-ons, are soft
limits. On {tiers.free.name}, the one organization is a soft limit too.

- A soft limit never stops a job, and never keeps a job from starting.
- Jobs keep running on every machine, also above the machines of your plan.
- Above a soft limit, every page shows a banner with the plan that covers
  your use. While a payment fails, the banner of the failed payment shows in
  its place.
- Passing a soft limit adds no charge.
- A new organization above the organizations of your plan joins inactive.
  Its jobs get no ciproxy runner while it is inactive. **Settings**, under
  **Organizations**, says why it waits.

## Change your plan

An Admin or a person with the Billing role changes the plan on the **Plans**
page.

- A move from {tiers.free.name} to a paid plan goes through Stripe
  Checkout, which takes cards. ciproxy changes the plan when Stripe confirms
  the payment.
- A move from {tiers.team.name} to {tiers.business.name} needs no checkout.
  Stripe charges the prorated difference to the saved card at once, and the
  plan changes when the charge succeeds. When the charge fails, the
  workspace keeps its plan, and the page offers to try again or to update
  the card.
- **Move to Free** takes effect at the end of the paid period. Jobs keep
  running, also above the soft limits of {tiers.free.name}.
- **Invoices and card** on **Plan and usage** opens the Stripe customer
  portal, where you see the invoices and change the card.
