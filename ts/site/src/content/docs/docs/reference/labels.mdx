Each job starts in a fresh VM of the size of its label: on your machines
first, and on Boat when they are busy.

## Default labels

Every workspace starts with these labels. `ciproxy` is the default label.

| Label                                       | Size                             | Slots |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ----- |
| `ciproxy` {jsx(Badge, { text: "Default" })} | 2 vCPU · 8 GB · 20 GB free disk  | 1     |
| `ciproxy-4vcpu`                             | 4 vCPU · 16 GB · 20 GB free disk | 2     |
| `ciproxy-8vcpu`                             | 8 vCPU · 32 GB · 60 GB free disk | 4     |

Like the disk of a GitHub-hosted runner, the disk of a label is the free
disk that each job gets. Its VM also holds the image: with the current
image, a job of `ciproxy` has a VM disk of about {defaultJobDiskGb} GB.

```yaml title=".github/workflows/e2e.yml"
jobs:
  e2e:
    runs-on: ciproxy-8vcpu
    steps:
      - uses: actions/checkout@v4
      - run: npm run test:e2e
```

A label sets the size of the VM, not its software. Every label starts from
the same image. To use other software, see
[Run steps in another image][container].

## Slots

Each machine keeps a quarter of its threads and its memory for the host. It
splits the rest into slots of 2 vCPU and 8 GB. A job takes as many slots as
its size needs, so a job of `ciproxy-8vcpu` takes 4 slots.

A running job also holds its VM disk. Each machine keeps {imageBudgetGb} GB
for the VM images, and a quarter of the rest for the host. The machine cache
uses the disk that the jobs leave.

The agent reports the slots of a machine when it connects. A Hetzner AX42
with {ax42.threads} threads and {ax42.memoryGb} GB has {machineSlots(ax42)}
slots.

## Custom labels

Add a label with its own size in **Settings**, under **Labels**. A label has
a name, vCPU, memory in GB, and free disk in GB, with at least 1 GB of free
disk. Sizes use decimal GB, so 1 GB is 1,000,000,000 bytes.

Each label runs on your machines, on Boat, or on both, with the switches
**Your machines** and **Boat**. A label that allows both starts its jobs on
your machines first.

- A label name follows the rules of GitHub for runner labels. ciproxy checks
  the name with GitHub when you save the label.
- A label keeps its name. To rename a label, add a new one, move your
  workflows to it, and delete the old one.
- You can delete any label except `ciproxy`. The waiting jobs of a deleted
  label stay queued in GitHub, and its running jobs finish.
- After you change the size of a label, each job that a runner takes gets
  the new size. A job that runs during the change keeps its VM.
- The dialog of a label says how many jobs of its size fit on each of your
  machines at a time.

:::note[Sizes larger than your machines]
A job whose size fits none of your machines runs on Boat only. When no Boat
size fits either, the label setting **When nothing fits** decides: its jobs
wait, or run on the largest Boat size.
:::

**Wait** is the default. **Run on the largest Boat size** can give a job
less vCPU, memory, or free disk than its label, but its disk always holds
the image.

## Where a job runs

A job that one of your machines can run waits there for room, up to the
overflow wait. Then it starts on Boat, when Boat can take it. The wait is
30 seconds by default. Set it in **Settings**, under **Overflow**: at once,
15 seconds, 30 seconds, 1 minute, or 2 minutes.

At the Boat spend limit, jobs run on your machines only, and wait there for
room.
