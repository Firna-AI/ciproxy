## How a job runs

1. GitHub queues a job whose workflow says `runs-on: ciproxy`.
2. ciproxy follows the [label order][label-order] to start a runner in a
   fresh VM. On your machines, a ready warm runner takes the job at once.
3. If your machines have no room, the job can go to the next place in its
   order, such as your Boat account. The wait for room on your machines
   is 30 seconds by default.
4. On your machines, `actions/cache` restores entries from the disk of the
   machine when it has them, and saves them there and to GitHub.

## What you need

- A GitHub organization with private repositories.
- One or more x64 Linux machines with systemd, cgroup v2, KVM, 4 cores,
  16 GB of memory, and 250 GB of disk or more, or Macs with Apple Silicon
  for macOS jobs.
  [Add a machine](/docs/guides/add-a-machine/#what-the-machine-needs) lists
  the supported distributions and what a Mac needs.
- For overflow, [connect a provider][connect] such as Boat. You can do
  this later.
