## How a job runs

1. GitHub queues a job whose workflow says `runs-on: ciproxy`.
2. A ready warm runner on one of your machines takes the job at once. Or
   ciproxy starts a runner where a machine has room. The job runs in a
   fresh VM.
3. When your machines have no room for the job, ciproxy can start a runner
   for it in your Boat account instead. By default, it waits 30 seconds
   for room on your machines first.
4. On your machines, `actions/cache` restores entries from the disk of the
   machine when it has them, and saves them there and to GitHub.

## What you need

- A GitHub organization with private repositories.
- One or more x64 Linux machines with systemd, cgroup v2, KVM, 4 cores,
  16 GB of memory, and 250 GB of disk or more.
  [Add a machine](/docs/guides/add-a-machine/#what-the-machine-needs) lists
  the supported distributions.
- A Boat account for overflow. You can connect it later.
